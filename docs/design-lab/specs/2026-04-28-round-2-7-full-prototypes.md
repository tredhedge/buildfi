# Round 2.7 — Full Lab Prototypes

Date: 2026-04-28  
Location: `design-lab/experiments/round-2-7-full-prototypes`

## Scope

This pass builds fuller isolated prototypes for:

- Guide 101
- Guide 201/301
- Decumulation tool
- Debt tool

All files are lab-only. No production guide or tool files are modified.

## Intent

Move from direction boards into fuller product-like pages that can be reviewed for:

- shell consistency
- pacing
- chapter/navigation quality
- Product vs Reading family separation
- interaction framing

## Product model being tested

### Reading family

- stronger cover / chapter rhythm
- sticky chapter rail
- calmer report-adjacent pacing
- instructional before interactive

### Product family

- shared dark/light product shell
- strong numeric emphasis
- exploration inside the page
- compact decisions and outcome summaries

## What these are not

- not production implementations
- not wired to real calculator engines
- not final copy
- not final responsive QA

## Review questions

1. Do the two guides feel like one coherent Reading family?
2. Do the two tools feel like one coherent Product family?
3. Does decum feel like the calmest Product surface?
4. Do the guides now feel close enough to the AI report language?
5. Are the tools coherent without becoming generic dashboards?
