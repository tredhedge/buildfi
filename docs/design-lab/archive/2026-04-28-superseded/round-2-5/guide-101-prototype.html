01

Your debt map

Start with clarity before optimization.

A 101 guide should feel approachable but still polished. The main move here is not more UI. It’s stronger reading cadence: fewer cues fighting for attention, more breathing room, and typography that feels closer to a professional note than an app export.

Key idea

Not all debt is equally urgent. The rate and structure matter more than the label.

Reader benefit

By the end of the section, the reader should know what to tackle first and why.
