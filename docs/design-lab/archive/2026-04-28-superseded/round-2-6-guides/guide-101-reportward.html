Part 101 · Foundational guide

See your debt clearly before trying to beat it.

A beginner guide should feel steady and credible. This version borrows the AI report’s calmer opening structure so the reader feels oriented before they meet any tactics.

Why this direction matters: the old guide shell read more like a polished app page. This version aims to read more like a short advisory booklet: fewer visual jumps, more chapter pacing, and stronger “what this means for you” language.

How this guide unfolds

The TOC shifts from a grid of utility cards toward a more report-like chapter list. The goal is to make the guide feel like a reading path, not a feature menu.

01

Map the balances first

Clarity is more useful than urgency in the first five minutes.

The first section should sound less like a UI tutorial and more like a thoughtful advisor note. Readers need to know why they are listing balances, not just be told to input them.

Most important question

Which balance is costing the most each month, not just which one feels the largest?

Reading-mode cue

Lead with understanding, then let the tool appear later if the reader wants a calculator.

02

Make the interest visible

The reader should feel the drag before they see a repayment method.

Guide 101 benefits from implication-first language. Instead of presenting raw mechanics first, the page should say what the interest does to flexibility, time, and emotional breathing room.

Editorial insight: a 101 guide becomes more trustworthy when the writing explains the real-life consequence before it shows the technical reason.
03

Choose one strategy you can stick with

The guide should make the decision feel manageable, not abstract.

This section should feel like a calm comparison memo. The reader does not need more surface complexity. They need one practical choice and confidence in what that choice optimizes.

Avalanche

Best if the reader values mathematical efficiency and wants to reduce total interest cost.

Snowball

Best if quick wins and momentum are more important than theoretical optimality.

What happens next

Guide first, tool second.

The guide should hand off to the debt tool only after the reader feels oriented. That is the difference between a publication voice and a product voice.
