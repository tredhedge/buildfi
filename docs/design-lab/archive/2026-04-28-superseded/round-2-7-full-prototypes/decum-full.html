Action guidance

Protect the bridge

Keep 2–3 years of spending in safe assets so market shocks do not force bad sales early in retirement.

Use tax bands deliberately

Pull enough from registered assets to avoid a large forced spike later, but not so much that you trigger unnecessary clawbacks now.

Keep optionality visible

The shell should make it easy to compare a stable baseline with a tested alternative without confusing the two.
