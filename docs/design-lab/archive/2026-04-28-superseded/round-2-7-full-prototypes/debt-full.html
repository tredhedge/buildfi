Payoff shape

The debt tool can be more visually assertive than decum, but it still benefits from a caption that translates the chart into a client action.

Immediate moves

Freeze the rate damage

Do not send new spending to the most expensive balance while repayment is underway.

Automate the extra

If the reader truly has monthly room, it should hit the target account automatically before it gets absorbed elsewhere.

Show the tradeoff plainly

Snowball can still be the right behavioral choice, but the shell should always reveal the cost of that preference.
