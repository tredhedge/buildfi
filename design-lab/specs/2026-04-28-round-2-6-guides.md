# Round 2.6 — Guide Refinement Recommendations

Date: 2026-04-28  
Scope: Guide 101 and Guide 201 only  
Location: `design-lab/experiments/round-2-6-guides`

## Goal

Push the guides closer to the AI report / Reading-view language without making them feel like static PDFs or overdesigned landing pages.

## Core Recommendation

The guides should live in the same editorial family as the AI reports, but in a lighter, more educational form:

- same serif/sans pairing
- same warm paper palette
- same calmer chapter rhythm
- less visible app/tool chrome
- stronger reading flow

## What should change from Round 2.5

### 1. Stronger report-like cover

- Replace simple guide hero blocks with a more report-like opening:
  - eyebrow
  - title
  - promise line
  - concise orientation copy

### 2. Chapter rail instead of flat guide cards

- Desktop guides should use a sticky chapter rail
- The current chapter should highlight while reading
- This makes the guides feel more like the AI report reading experience

### 3. Fewer “tiles,” more paper rhythm

- Reduce the sense that the guide is a sequence of widgets
- Let sections feel like paced chapters on paper rather than product cards

### 4. 101 should feel guided, not dense

- Simpler section count
- More “what this means for you” framing
- One practical action at the end of each section

### 5. 201 should feel richer, not just longer

- More depth and nuance than 101
- Still restrained
- Bonus / interactive references should appear later in the reading path, not near the top

## What should stay different from the AI report

- Guides remain instructional, not client-personalized
- Fewer KPI blocks than the reports
- Less premium ceremony than the reports
- Less simulation language early on

## Success criteria

- Guide 101 and 201 feel like part of the same Reading family as the reports
- 101 stays accessible
- 201 feels more advanced without turning dashboard-like
- The guide shell looks publication-grade, not tool-grade
